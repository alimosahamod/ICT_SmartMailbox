Most trips end at an empty box

A brass bank in a lobby, a steel wall in a high-rise, a post at the end of a long drive. Wherever the box is, the routine is the same: you walk there, you look, and usually there is nothing new.

In rain, heat or darkness, on stairs or with a walking stick, that walk costs real effort. People start checking every few days instead, and bills or official letters sit unseen.

Questions, answered

Does it work with the mailbox I already have?

Yes. The module clips or sticks to the inside wall of most roadside, wall-mounted and lobby mailboxes. Buildings can add the lock module for parcel lockers and remote opening.

Do I need Wi-Fi at home?

No. Each module has its own low-power mobile connection (LTE-M or NB-IoT), so it works at the end of a drive or in a basement lobby where Wi-Fi does not reach.

Will flyers and advertising set it off?

It reports anything resting inside the box, so a flyer counts as mail. You can mute alerts for a weekday your area gets advertising, and the status still shows what is inside.

How do I let a neighbour open my box?

In the app, choose Access, pick the person and set a start and end time. They get a code that only works inside that window. You see each opening in your history.

What happens to parcels that do not fit?

In buildings with a parcel room, the caretaker scans the label, stores the parcel on a free shelf and the app tells you where it is, with a pickup code.

How long does the battery last?

About two years in normal use. The app warns you weeks before it runs low, and the pack is replaceable without tools.

Is there a camera?

No. Smart Mailbox detects that something is inside, not what it is. Nothing is filmed and nothing about your mail's content is stored.

What will it cost?

The plan is a one-time kit plus a small monthly fee that covers the mobile connection and support. Final prices will be shared with the waitlist first.
