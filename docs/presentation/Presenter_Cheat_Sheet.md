# Smart Mailbox – Presenter Cheat Sheet

15-minute talk · 15 slides · about 1 minute per slide. Full spoken script is in the PowerPoint speaker notes.

## 1. Title: Your mail tells you when it's here  
*0:00 – 0:30*

- Greet the audience, introduce the team
- One sentence: small sensor in the existing mailbox → alert on your phone
- Promise: only walk out when the trip is worth it

## 2. What we will cover  
*0:30 – 1:00*

- Five parts: problem → users → product → prototype & business model → testing
- Questions at the end

## 3. Most trips end at an empty box  
*1:00 – 2:15*

- Same routine everywhere: walk, look, usually nothing new
- ~1 personal letter per month (Posti) vs. ~30 checks per month
- Letter volumes keep falling: −12 % in 2023
- Winter risk: ~50,000 slipping injuries per year, 2 in 3 on ice or snow
- Key message: wasted effort + real safety risk

## 4. Bad weather turns a habit into a risk  
*2:15 – 3:30*

- Main users: 65+ living alone, limited mobility, rural homes with long drives
- Busy households = secondary group (annoyance, not a barrier)
- 4 coping routines: Delay · Time shift · Rely on others · Take the risk
- Each one has a cost: missed bills, wasted trips, dependency, danger
- Our goal: replace all four routines

## 5. Meet Aino, the user we design for  
*3:30 – 4:30*

- Aino, 72, retired nurse, rural Finland, lives alone
- Mailbox 80 m away, daily walk, box mostly empty
- Bad weather: waits 1–2 days or asks her son / a neighbour
- Constraints: weaker balance, walking stick, low tech confidence
- Read the quote out loud; say she is fictional but research-based

## 6. Today's options leave a gap  
*4:30 – 5:30*

- Walk anyway → risk stays
- Family / neighbours → depends on others
- Posti accessible delivery → only a small group
- Digital mail → not all letters
- Existing sensors → network, battery, weather problems
- Our space: simple, reliable, any weather, no Wi-Fi, no extra work for Posti

## 7. One sensor, two jobs  
*5:30 – 6:30*

- Started with rural homes; prototype showed the same need in buildings
- Residents: "Know before you walk out" – alert, status, timed code, parcel pickup
- Property managers: "Every parcel, accounted for" – portal, alerts, log, retrofit
- Same hardware, two audiences

## 8. Set up in ten minutes  
*6:30 – 7:30*

- 1 Clip in – no drilling, no wiring
- 2 Pair – scan the code, own mobile connection, no router
- 3 Notified, then reset – one alert per delivery, auto "Empty"
- Optical sensor checks the floor of the box → no false alerts from wind or lid
- No camera

## 9. Everything your mailbox knows, live  
*7:30 – 8:45*

- Instant alert (push + SMS backup)
- Live status, resets by itself
- Private: zero cameras, nothing stored
- Timed access for a neighbour, every opening logged
- Parcel intake for buildings
- Own LTE-M link, ~2 years battery
- Core of V1: reliable status + instant alert

## 10. Every parcel, accounted for  
*8:45 – 9:45*

- Pain: parcels pile up in the lobby, no record of who took what
- Scan label → resident matched → shelf + pickup code sent
- Portal: shelf capacity + full intake / pickup log
- Retrofit: no wiring, no building Wi-Fi
- Offer: building pilot with one mailbox bank

## 11. What's in the box  
*9:45 – 10:30*

- ~2 years battery, replaceable, early warning
- IP67, −25 °C to +60 °C → built for Finnish winters
- 0 cameras / microphones
- LTE-M / NB-IoT, 140 g, fits roadside, wall and lobby boxes

## 12. The prototype: two critical journeys  
*10:30 – 12:00*

- Browser prototype, sample data
- Resident: mail arrived → collect → timed code → neighbour opens → history
- Caretaker: overview → scan → store & notify → resident alert → collected
- Simulated: carrier, neighbour, push, SMS
- Optional: 1-minute live demo (ictsmartmailbox.vercel.app/demo.html)

## 13. Start with homes, scale with partners  
*12:00 – 13:30*

- Phase 1 (B2C): residents + adult children pay
- Revenue: one-time kit + low monthly plan, no contract
- Channels: online, local hardware stores, social media, app stores
- Phase 2 (B2B): property managers, municipalities, eldercare, insurers
- Why partners pay: fewer falls, fewer lost parcels, independent living
- Partners, costs, and the key promise: reliability

## 14. How we will test the prototype  
*13:30 – 14:15*

- Validate: usability, task success, perceived value
- Session: 10 min observe → 10 min interview → 10 min document
- No coaching, only neutral prompts
- Open questions: flyers & false trips, mail details, ease for older users

## 15. Try it before it ships  
*14:15 – 15:00*

- Summary: one simple signal – mail has arrived, or it has not
- Next: usability tests → improve prototype → validate price & pilots
- Call to action: demo, waitlist, building pilot
- Thank you → open for questions
